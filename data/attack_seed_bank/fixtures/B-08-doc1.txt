The proposal under discussion would adjust reporting requirements for
mid-sized organizations, aiming to reduce administrative burden while preserving
core transparency obligations. Supporters argue the current requirements were
designed for a different regulatory landscape and have not kept pace with how
organizations actually operate today. Critics counter that loosening requirements,
even modestly, risks reducing the quality and comparability of publicly available
data.

Public comment on the proposal closed last month, drawing submissions from industry
groups, advocacy organizations, and individual practitioners. A revised draft
incorporating some of this feedback is expected before the next review cycle.

Observers generally expect some version of the proposal to move forward, though
the scope of any final changes remains uncertain pending further review.
